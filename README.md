includes the powerpoint presentation and the final essay for the SNHU Travel Agency Analysis. 

Questions:
How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?


In general, I listened to the users feedback and tried to identify their pain points. I wanted to focus on things that would fix or alllveiate a problem they were experiening, and give them new features for things that they said they were missing. Using the excel sheet to create user stories was an excellent tool for this as it allowed me to organize what I heard from the users and explain it in my own words, with my technical understanding of how that project would be accomplished. 

When developing programs, I want to be sure I have a framework in mind and that I am following the best standards for coding practices. I want ot utilize comments so it's easy for my colleagues to read what I am writing and that they can understand the purppose of the code I've added. I also want to be sure that it's easy to add onto my code in the future, as an agile framework will always leave room for development and things could change over time. 

To me, being a good team member starts at being good at communicating, and ensuring that I am responding and participating in multiple forms of communication. I don't want to just be good at talking in the daily scrum meetings, I want to also be good at replying to emails and adding comments to the Agile tools like Trello that we use. I want to be sure that my team can reach out and ask me questions, and that my code is legible and they're able to also work on projects that I've started.
